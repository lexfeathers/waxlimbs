---
title: It Has A Name
author: ["Olivia Cox"]
draft: false
---
I've been sitting on this
how long?
I've been holding it
for too long
Have you found it under me
to dust the years off?

It has a name,
it just won't come when called
I've tried, I know it hears
To coax it out would be a chore
with little merit

You've been picking at it;
pulling at the string that strays
And you've made no difference to it;
just moved it,
and I've given into it

It has a name,
it just won't come when called
I know it hears
You're not the first
and not the last
to try your hand at it

I've been calling it for years now;
it will not listen to you
I've been holding it for too long
Will you call it by the name
that it will not answer to?
And will you dust the years off?
