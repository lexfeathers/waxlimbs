---
title: Fire At Last
author: ["Lex Feathers"]
draft: false
---
Floating ember bath
dissolving, curious
Becoming fire at last
Cinder heart,
dissolving, furious

Only took a spark
igniting, immolate
Lifting looks from glass
Whispers burned
Exploding, insidious
Fire at last

Bleeding, liquid hearts
beating, twisting
Every word
Hungry wraith
Devouring, humility
