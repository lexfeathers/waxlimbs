Early morning, saw you walk beneath the trees
and behind you, dragged an elk along the leaves

Haven't seen you; left for higher altitudes
Dusty picture of a life in solitude

I see the heaviness
Stones laid with every breath

Keep the heavy stone inside
covering the hole in you that's open wide
Hide the heavy stone inside
covering that void in you that's still alive

Calling and you
Waste away
Reaching out
through the haze
slow decay
Calling and you
Waste away
Reaching out
through the haze
slow decay

Calling and you
Waste away
