I wandered into the trees at night
In hopes of seeing them
Seeing them there
I hold my breath
And bite my lip
I'm pulled beyond the seam

In the presence of hidden voices
That yell and laugh and cry
I'm driven here by choices
Without a reason why

There will be no going home
You will never know the truth
Now that branches hold the bones
Now your blood belongs to roots
