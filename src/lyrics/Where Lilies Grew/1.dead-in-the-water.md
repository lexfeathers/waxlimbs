title: Dead In The Water
author: ["Lex Feathers", "Olivia Cox"]
draft: false
---
I take a breath
I grip the edge
devils won't find me out here

the blackened sky
the whitecaps fly
and the water is warmer than the air

what can you do?
where are your friends now?
where's your head now?
you feel it under you
the black below falls
they know you're here now

the ocean feeds
the dead beneath
can you feel them float up from the deep?

what can you do?
where are your friends now?
where's your head now?
you feel them under you
the black below calls
and they are here now
