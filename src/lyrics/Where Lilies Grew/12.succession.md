I lay beside you
Ignited, for the thousandth time
Who will light it
When there's no one left alive

I beg forgiveness for forgetting

With all the years gone by
Alone and lost in time
This skin belongs to me
But I'll lie in flames and soon be free

All you've known, all you've seen
Falls to ash, grows (fades) to green
In the age of rebirth
You'll return, roots and earth

I beg forgiveness for forgetting

I staggered in through the trees
To a place where lilies grow
To a place that I did know
In an age so long ago

I fell to my knees
In a place where lilies grow
Where ashes found their home

I sank through the leaves
in that place where lilies grew
near a thousand years ago

I can't be blamed
There's no remembrance in after days
When the distance is an age
and the years have changed our face
