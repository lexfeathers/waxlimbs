This is new light
Autumn moon night
Catching all your visitors
and raising them like children
Their wings will beat against the jar
lighting the way from where you are

Kill the headlights
Onyx mere of night
They won't survive in there
Seeing that look in your eyes,
holding the pedal to drive

Once you crash, you are front and center
Spit and thrash, as if you think that's better
At the impact
throw your head back
