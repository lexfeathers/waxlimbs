Caught it in the mirror
but I can't make it out
Nightmares are clearer
getting bolder now

Draw blood from the mirror;
let it empty out

What will come to pass
shimmers in the flow
Fill the cracks; the only way I'll know

A calamity
I saw all of this
below

Here I am
in my stead

What good is the glass
when you're falling in
and you've yet to see the end?
What good is the glass
when you're falling in
and it's yet to come to pass?
