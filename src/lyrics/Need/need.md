The end of era buries me in leaves
heavy with grief
Lost in the changes that wash in from the sea
Lost in my need

I won't change for you;
you'll have to break me.
Even with my heart aflame
and lost in my need.

A prick of feathers,
a lightness in my head;
humbled by pain
Words from the future that held me in my stead
Lost in the flame

I won't change for you;
you'll have to break me.
Even with my heart aflame
and lost in my need.

I won't lose myself
I will be permanent!
Even if identity's at odds with my grief.

(I've got a new reflection and it's eating me)
(I've got a new reflection and it rules my need)

My burning era falls out from from under me;
crumbles like leaves.
Reform the mirror and smile through the tears,
guard well my grief.
