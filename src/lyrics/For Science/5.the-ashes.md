---
title: The Ashes
author: ["Lex Feathers"]
draft: false
---
Beyond the borders this year
Straying to the edges
We found the homes of giants

We cleared out the trees
We cast them down in envy
Scorched into the deeps
'till all the earth was burning

Daylight turning silver
Changes in the heavens
The stars would flare in anger

The sea came rushing in
The earth was torn asunder
Mountains pouring flame
The deeps would even shudder

Everything's forgotten
A pool of time laid empty
The light of stories burning

The whites of blinded eyes
The spell of cedar dying
A thousand ugly cries
and all sound seemed to end
The sea came rushing in
The earth was torn asunder
Mountains pouring flames
The ashes of our Mother
