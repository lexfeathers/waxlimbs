Your main intention was not mistake
You choke the room with smoke and whiskey taste
Romantic views of past now cancerous intake
Your main intention was not mistake

And I'm sure there you saw
Many faces in the fog
So high up, wrapt in her arms but
You are bare, you are disarmed

Your main intention was not mistake
The coercion coming closer;
the frozen grasp, the gasp
As fingers twine in shirt.
Caelum cavea ardens est
But she doesn't mention them
Nor the swirling thoughts of her duress
You've pulled yourself into this mess

The branches, their cold subtlety
Covered in frost
Their cold subtlety
Their frigid adultery
