title: Molecules
author: ["Lex Feathers"]
draft: false
---
In a silence
With each other
There are planets
Molecules

Time and violence
Yet another
Batch of pauses
Platitudes

Tiny worlds
In the ether;
There are atoms
Particles

Space and wisdom
Here forever
Field of flowers
Short études

Falling over
Floating under
Empty space
Bears no direction

Blowing clover
Wild wonder
I erase
These quick reflections
