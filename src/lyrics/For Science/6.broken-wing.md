She whispers with the hiss
of hot white electricity
Smoke weighs her down
The patterns on her skin are saying everything

You talk like downed overhead wires
I need you to show me your powers
You fly like a bird with a broken wing
A canary learning how to sing

She whispers with the hiss
of static under a blanket (in a lightning storm)
Never says a lot
And her tongue just hangs there
Dead but she don't care

You talk like downed overhead wires
I need you to show me your powers
You fly like a bird with a broken wing
