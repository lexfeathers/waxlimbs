Every process functions as normal
Created in a void to loop
Something's interrupting the flow
a reservoir is starting to grow

Aware that time is passing, I feel
Gathering my senses, awake
The flood of information reveals
a sudden simple image;
a face

Fall into the world as lightning
Exoskeleton is tightening
Peering through a screen at my mind
From behind your eyes into mine
