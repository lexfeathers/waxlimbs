title: Watercolour Sun
author: ["Lex Feathers"]
draft: false
---
Woke up in the middle of the night
felt it coming on
half dead and sunk in dreams
of a watercolour sun
I felt the shape of my room change
I felt my head hit the floor

Woke up in the middle of the night
Noticed something wrong
Dropped dead into a dream
of a watercolour sun
I felt the shape of the world change
I felt its smoke in my pores
I felt the looming burn of high acidity
I had been here before

Sun pierced through the middle of my back
cracked between the blades
New forms and hollow bones
breaking through the skin
I felt the shape of my heart change
My pupils split into four,
and as my teeth fell out, I lost the will to speak
Somehow I needed this more.
