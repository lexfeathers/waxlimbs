title: Chase The Moon
author: ["Lex Feathers", "Paul Geldart", "Will Jarvis"]

---
I watch the water rise from town
across the river, boats cast off
the little kind, you leave behind
when autumn rings the bell

A coat of leaves to hide yourself
I chase the moon across the sky
Above the dewdrop fields I ride
by fireflies, their points of light,
I navigate the night
my flooded heart will rise
In time,
my flooded heart will rise

The leaves decay in red and brown
the same within me and without
and every dead and broken thing
in memories on the ground
the loudness of that sound
surrounds, within me and without
Surrounds, within me and without
