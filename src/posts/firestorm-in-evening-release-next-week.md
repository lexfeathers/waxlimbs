## Hello world,

we have a new single to announce!

For a while now we've been chipping away at songs for a new album. It's been a slow, methodical process with plenty of ups and downs, but we're very happy with the songs we've been writing. Like most groups of creative people, we work best with deadlines, so we're setting some.

The first is October 2nd 2026 which will mark the release of the album's first single: _Firestorm In Evening._ This is a song about an approaching end of sorts, so it feels kind of tongue-in-cheek to release it first.

We've played _Firestorm_ live a bunch over the last year, and through those proving-grounds have honed a song we're very proud of. Clocking in at 6 minutes, we've taken a more "progressive rock" approach than usual and have let ourselves loosen up a bit with the arrangement of the song.

I don't want to say too much before the release next Friday, so here are links to our [Bandcamp](https://waxlimbs.bandcamp.com/), [Ampwall](https://ampwall.com/a/waxlimbs) and [Mirlo](https://mirlo.space/waxlimbs) pages where you can follow to get notified when it goes up.

Looking forward to sharing this with you all!
